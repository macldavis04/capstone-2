MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES, INC.
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK: WORKPLACE POLICIES AND BENEFITS
Document owner: People Team
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: January 1, 2026

1. ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK

This handbook summarizes the workplace policies and benefits available to full-time Meridian employees, including corporate and retail staff. Part-time retail employees and contractors should check with the People team for the benefits that apply to them. Where this handbook and a formal plan document differ, the plan document controls.

2. WORKING ARRANGEMENTS

2.1 Corporate offices
Corporate employees work from their assigned campus (Cupertino Park, Austin, Cork, Shanghai, or Singapore) at least 3 days a week, on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays. Many hardware and product teams work on site 5 days a week because their work requires secure labs.

2.2 Policy change in 2025
In April 2025, Meridian moved corporate staff from a 2-day to a 3-day office requirement and removed most fully remote roles. Feedback was negative in several departments, and the People team committed to reviewing the policy after the 2026 engagement survey.

2.3 Retail stores
Meridian Store employees work scheduled shifts published at least 2 weeks in advance. During launch weeks and the holiday quarter (mid-November through December), stores extend hours and time-off requests may be limited. Retail employees working on a launch day receive a launch-day bonus.

2.4 Launch periods
Teams supporting a hardware launch may be asked to work extended hours in the 4 weeks before the event. Managers must give compensating time off within 60 days after launch.

3. TIME OFF

- Paid time off (PTO): 15 days per year in the first 3 years, 20 days from year 4, and 25 days from year 8. Up to 5 unused days carry over.
- Company holidays: 9 per year, set by region, plus a winter shutdown for corporate staff in the last week of December.
- Sick leave: up to 10 paid days per year, separate from PTO.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for birthing parents and 6 weeks fully paid for non-birthing parents, after 6 months of service.
- Bereavement leave: up to 5 paid days.

4. COMPENSATION AND REVIEWS

- Performance reviews happen once a year, in October.
- Salary adjustments and stock refreshes are granted in December, based on the October review.
- Meridian targets base pay at the 50th to 60th percentile of the local market for retail and support roles, and the 75th percentile for engineering and design roles.
- In fiscal year 2025, merit increases for retail and support roles were capped at 2.5 percent. Employees in Retail and Customer Support raised this in survey comments.

5. BENEFITS

- Health coverage: medical, dental, and vision plans, with Meridian paying 90 percent of premiums for employees and 75 percent for dependents.
- Retirement: matching contributions of up to 5 percent of salary.
- Employee stock purchase plan: buy Meridian shares at a 15 percent discount through payroll.
- Product discount: 25 percent off Meridian products, up to a yearly limit, and a free Meridian Cloud subscription.
- Learning budget: $1,000 per employee per year.
- Employee assistance program: free, confidential counseling for employees and household members.

6. RECOGNITION

- Meridian Spotlight: peers nominate colleagues each quarter. Winners receive a $500 bonus.
- Launch recognition: teams that ship a product receive a commemorative award and a team celebration.
- Service anniversaries: additional stock grant at 5 years and a paid 4-week sabbatical at 10 years.

7. CAREER DEVELOPMENT

- Every employee has a career conversation with their manager at least twice a year.
- Internal job postings are open to employees with at least 12 months in their current role.
- Retail employees can apply to the Store to Corporate program, which offers 10 rotational roles per region each year.
- Published promotion criteria exist for Engineering, Design, and Sales. Criteria for Retail and Customer Support are still in draft.

8. EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT SURVEY

The People team runs an engagement survey every year in the fourth quarter. Employees rate their overall satisfaction on a 1 to 10 scale and answer questions about manager support, workload, growth, and recognition. Results are reported by department, with no group under 5 people reported separately. Department leaders must share results and publish an action plan within 45 days.

Survey themes from 2025 that leadership committed to address: launch and holiday workload in Retail and Customer Support, the 3-day office requirement, the 2.5 percent merit cap for retail and support roles, and missing promotion criteria outside Engineering, Design, and Sales.

9. CONDUCT

All employees must follow the Code of Conduct and the Information Security and Confidentiality Policy, including product secrecy rules. Concerns about workplace behavior can be raised with a manager, the People team, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
