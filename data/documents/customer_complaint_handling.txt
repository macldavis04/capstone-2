MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES, INC.
CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCEDURE
Document owner: VP of Customer Experience
Version: 5.1
Effective date: February 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE

This procedure explains how Meridian receives, tracks, resolves, and learns from customer complaints. A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction from a customer about our products, services, repairs, billing, or staff, whether or not the customer uses the word "complaint." Meridian's brand promise is a premium experience from purchase to repair, so how we handle a complaint matters as much as the product itself. This procedure covers individual consumers and Meridian for Business accounts.

2. GUIDING PRINCIPLES

- Acknowledge fast, even before the answer is known.
- One owner per complaint from intake to close.
- Fix the customer's problem first, then fix the root cause.
- Never argue about whether the customer's frustration is valid.
- Treat every customer's data with care; never ask for a device passcode.

3. INTAKE CHANNELS

Customers can raise complaints through:
- Meridian Stores, at the Tech Desk
- The Meridian Support app and website chat (available 24/7)
- Phone support (24/7 for consumers; business hours for Meridian for Business)
- Email to support@meridian.example
- Their account manager, for Meridian for Business customers
- Social media and review sites, monitored by the Community team

Every complaint must be logged as a case in the support system within 30 minutes of receipt, tagged with the "complaint" label, the product, and the customer's region (Americas, Europe, Greater China, or Rest of Asia Pacific).

4. SEVERITY TIERS AND SERVICE LEVELS

Support assigns a tier at intake. The tier can be raised at any time.

Tier 1, Critical
Definition: A safety concern (overheating, swelling battery, injury), a suspected account compromise or data exposure, or a business customer with more than 50 devices unable to work.
First response: within 15 minutes
Resolution or workaround target: within 4 hours
Updates to customer: every 60 minutes until resolved
Safety complaints are also reported to Product Safety within 1 hour.

Tier 2, High
Definition: A device that will not power on or is unusable, data loss, a repair that failed or was not returned on time, or a billing error over $500.
First response: within 1 hour
Resolution target: within 1 business day; a replacement or loaner device is offered if a repair will take longer than 3 days

Tier 3, Standard
Definition: A feature not working as expected with a workaround, subscription billing disputes, or dissatisfaction with a store or support interaction.
First response: within 4 business hours
Resolution target: within 3 business days

Tier 4, Low
Definition: Feedback, feature requests framed as complaints, or cosmetic issues.
First response: within 1 business day
Resolution target: acknowledged and routed to Product within 5 business days

5. HANDLING STEPS

Step 1, Acknowledge. Respond within the tier's first-response time. Confirm the issue in your own words and tell the customer when they will hear from us next.
Step 2, Investigate. Run remote diagnostics with the customer's consent, check for known issues, and review repair and purchase history.
Step 3, Resolve. Repair, replace, refund, or provide a workaround. Confirm the problem is solved before closing.
Step 4, Close. Record the root cause category (hardware defect, software defect, repair experience, billing, store experience, or other) and send the post-resolution survey.
Step 5, Follow up. For Tier 1 and Tier 2 complaints, a senior advisor contacts the customer within 5 business days of closure.

6. ESCALATION PATH

- A missed first-response target escalates the case to the support team lead.
- A missed resolution target escalates to the Support Manager, and for business accounts, to the account manager.
- Tier 1 cases unresolved after 4 hours escalate to the VP of Customer Experience.
- Any Meridian for Business customer who threatens to cancel is flagged as a churn risk and handed to Customer Success under the churn-prevention playbook.
- Complaints alleging discrimination, harassment by a Meridian employee, or a privacy violation go directly to Legal, Privacy, and HR, bypassing the normal tiers.

7. REMEDIES AND GOODWILL

- Store and support advisors may offer goodwill up to $100 (a credit, accessory, or free month of Meridian Cloud) without approval.
- Goodwill from $100 to $1,000, including out-of-warranty replacements, requires a Support Manager or Store Leader.
- Anything above $1,000 requires the regional Customer Experience director.
- Customers with Meridian Care coverage receive repairs under its terms; advisors may not charge a covered customer.

8. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

- Use plain language, not technical jargon.
- Apologize for the impact without blaming other teams, carriers, or the customer.
- Never comment on unreleased products or future software features.
- Public complaints on social media get a public acknowledgment within 2 hours, then move to a private channel.

9. REPORTING AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

- Customer Experience publishes a weekly complaint report showing volume by tier, product, channel, and region, along with SLA attainment.
- Target SLA attainment is 95 percent for first response and 90 percent for resolution.
- Any root cause appearing in more than 50 complaints in a month becomes a ticket for the owning product team.
- Complaint volume always spikes in the 3 weeks after a major launch and during the holiday quarter; staffing plans account for this.
